Our Story

A Family Home
Above the Valley

Perched on the edge of the Great Rift Valley escarpment, Kilima Resort is a family-run retreat in Iten — Kenya's celebrated high-altitude training capital. Our hosts Keon and Florence welcome you into a home where incredible food, honest hospitality, and one of East Africa's most breathtaking views come together naturally.

Whether you're an athlete chasing altitude gains, a traveller seeking silence, or a family wanting to experience highland Kenya, Kilima is your base.

  • Free high-speed WiFi
  • Buffet & gluten-free breakfast
  • Airport transfers available
  • Free secure parking
  • Garden & terrace
  • Kids' club on-site
  • Daily housekeeping
  • Satellite TV in rooms

Beyond the Room

Iten's Best
Experiences

🏔

Escarpment Hiking

A dramatic 7 km trail drops 1,150 vertical metres down the escarpment into the Kerio Valley floor. Our hosts know every route — ask for a guide or a hand-drawn map.

🚴

Highland Cycling

Ride the same roads as Kenya's elite marathon runners. Rental bikes available; routes range from gentle valley trails to serious climbing circuits.

🌅

Sunrise Over the Rift

Step onto your terrace at dawn and watch light flood the Great Rift Valley. Guests consistently call it one of the most beautiful sunrises of their lives.

🍳

Home-Cooked Meals

Florence's breakfasts — legendary banana bread, fresh fruit, eggs to order — have become a reason in themselves to visit. Dinner on request; all meals are homemade.
